ABOUT ROADSENSE

Intelligent Monitoring for Campus Roads

RoadSense combines computer vision and audio detection to help authorized university personnel monitor campus road activity and manage recorded traffic violations.

Vehicle Detection

Detects and tracks vehicles passing through the monitored campus gate using a camera.

Speed Monitoring

Estimates vehicle speed and records detected speeding events for authorized review.

License Plate Recognition

Reads vehicle plate numbers and matches them against verified registered vehicles.

Horn Detection

Uses audio detection to identify possible horn events and preserve recordings for review.

SMS Notifications

Sends violation notifications to verified drivers through the configured SMS service.

Violation Management

Helps Security Services review evidence, manage appeals, and track progressive campus offenses.

HOW IT WORKS

From Detection to Review

RoadSense supports the campus monitoring process through three main steps.

01

Monitor

The roadside detector uses a camera and microphone to monitor vehicle movement, speed, license plates, and possible horn events.

02

Record

When a possible violation is detected, RoadSense captures supporting photo, video, and audio evidence and sends the record to the web portal.

03

Review

Authorized personnel review violation records, verify details, manage appeals, and handle notifications according to campus procedures.

Building Safer Campus Roads.
