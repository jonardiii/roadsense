Intelligent monitoring for campus roads

RoadSense combines computer vision and audio detection to help authorized university personnel monitor road activity and manage recorded violations.

Vehicle detection

Detects and tracks vehicles passing through the monitored campus gate.

Speed monitoring

Estimates vehicle speed and records speeding events for authorized review.

License plate recognition

Reads plate numbers and matches them against verified registered vehicles.

Horn detection

Uses audio detection to identify possible horn events and keep recordings for review.

SMS notifications

Sends violation notices to verified drivers through the configured SMS service.

Violation management

Helps Security Services review evidence, handle appeals, and track repeat offenses.

From detection to review

Every record follows the same three steps, from the roadside to the web portal.

  1. 1

    Monitor

    The roadside detector uses a camera and microphone to watch vehicle movement, speed, license plates, and possible horn events.

  2. 2

    Record

    When a possible violation is detected, RoadSense captures photo, video, and audio evidence and sends the record to the web portal.

  3. 3

    Review

    Authorized personnel check the details, handle appeals, and send notifications following campus procedures.

Built for the people who use campus roads

One portal, with the right view for each group.

Security Services

Review evidence, confirm violations, and decide on appeals from one queue.

Drivers

Register your vehicle, get notified of violations by SMS, and see the evidence behind each record.

Administrators

Manage accounts and keep the system running according to campus policy.

Building safer campus roads.

RoadSense supports Bukidnon State University's efforts to monitor campus traffic and promote responsible road use.
